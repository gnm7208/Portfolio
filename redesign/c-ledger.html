Income record

Kipato

Offline-first income record for informal workers. Log daily cash with no signal, import M-PESA history, generate a verifiable income statement a SACCO or lender can open without an account. An IndexedDB outbox queues offline writes and reconciles on reconnect.

I build projects from the ground up: planning, design, implementation, and deployment. My dream? To help carve out a name for Africa in software and robotics. (Blame the Transformers movies. I do.)
